Mechanics of Solids Lab

Perform any 10 Experiments

  1. Perform Tension Test on Mild Steel/ Aluminium on Universal Testing machine as per IS432 (I) To estimate hardness of water by EDTA method.
  2. Perform Compression test on Cast Iron on Universal Testing Machine as per IS 14858.
  3. Perform direct Shear Test on Mild Steel using Universal Testing Machine as per IS 5242.
  4. Determine Young's Modulus of Elasticity of different materials beam simply supported at ends.
  5. Calculate Impact Value/Toughness of Mild Steel and Aluminium using Izod and Charpy Impact Test Apparatus as per IS 1757.
  6. Perform Brinell, Rockwell and Vicker's hardness test on different metals.
  7. Estimate Maximum Bending moment and Shear force for simply supported and cantilever beams under point load and UDL using 'Combined Shear Force and Bending Moment' apparatus.
  8. Measure flexural rigidity (EI) for beam using 'Slope and Deflection' apparatus Identification of organic substances and their functional groups and compare it with theoretical value.
  9. Investigate the effect of beam length and width on deflection of beam and compare it with theoretical value using 'Slope and Deflection' apparatus.
  10. Perform the torsion test on wire/ Rod of different materials using 'Torsion of Bar' apparatus. (Part I) as per IS 1717.
  11. Measure the buckling load of three different slenderness ratio long columns of same lengths using 'Behaviour of column and struts' apparatus.
