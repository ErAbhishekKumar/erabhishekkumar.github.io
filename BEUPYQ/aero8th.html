Aircraft Maintenance, Repair and Overhaul [116802]

  • Unit 1.0: Introduction to Aircraft Maintenance & Regulatory Framework
    • Maintenance philosophy: preventive, corrective, predictive
    • Maintenance schedules (A, B, C, D checks)
    • Airworthiness requirements (DGCA, FAA, EASA)
    • Aircraft documentation: logbooks, Minimum Equipment List (MEL), and Aircraft Maintenance Manual (AMM)
    • Human factors in maintenance
    • Continuing Airworthiness Management Organization (CAMO)
  • Unit 2.0: Inspection & Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)
    • Inspection methods: visual, detailed, special NDT techniques
    • Ultrasonic testing
    • Radiography
    • Magnetic particle inspection
    • Dye penetrant testing and Eddy current testing
    • Corrosion detection and prevention
    • Structural health monitoring
    • Aircraft structural damage assessment
  • Unit 3.0: Aircraft Systems & Engine Overhaul
    • Hydraulic system maintenance
    • Landing gear maintenance
    • Electrical and avionics troubleshooting
    • Fuel system maintenance
    • Gas turbine engine maintenance and overhaul
    • Engine performance trend monitoring
  • Unit 4.0: Structural Repair Techniques
    • Metallic structure repairs (riveting, patching)
    • Composite repair procedures
    • Repair design approval process
    • Fatigue and crack growth management
    • Aircraft aging and life extension programs
    • Repair documentation and certification
  • Unit 5.0: MRO Management & Quality Assurance
    • MRO organizational structure
    • Maintenance planning & logistics
    • Safety Management Systems (SMS)
    • Digital MRO & predictive maintenance technologies
  • Unit 6.0: MRO Management & Quality Assurance
    • Quality control and audits
    • Reliability centered maintenance (RCM)

Flight Dynamics [116803]

  • Unit 1.0: Principles of Flight
    • Physical properties and structure of the atmosphere
    • International Standard Atmosphere (ISA)
    • Temperature-pressure-altitude relationships
    • Forces and moments on a flight vehicle
    • Equilibrium conditions
    • Equations of motion of a rigid flight vehicle
    • Measurement of airspeed: true, indicated, calibrated, and equivalent
    • Streamlined and bluff bodies
    • Types of drag in airplanes
    • Drag polar
    • Methods of drag reduction
  • Unit 2.0: Aircraft Performance in Level, Climbing, and Gliding Flight
    • Straight and level flight: thrust required vs. available
    • Power required vs. available
    • Effect of altitude on thrust and power
    • Conditions for minimum drag and minimum power
    • Climbing flight: maximum rate of climb
    • Steepest angle of climb
    • Gliding flight: minimum rate of sink
    • Shallowest angle of glide
    • Range and endurance for propeller- and jet-powered aircraft
  • Unit 3.0: Accelerating Flight
    • Take-off performance: ground roll, lift-off, climb-out
    • Landing performance: approach, flare, and ground roll
    • Turning performance: horizontal and vertical turns
    • Pull-up and pull-down maneuvers
    • Maximum turn rate and minimum turn radius
    • V-n diagram: maneuvering and gust load factors
  • Unit 4.0: Longitudinal Stability and Control
    • Degrees of freedom of an aircraft
    • Static and dynamic stability concepts
    • Static longitudinal stability: contributions of components
    • Neutral point, static margin
    • Hinge moment and elevator control effectiveness
    • Power effects
    • Elevator angle to trim
    • Stick force gradient
    • Aerodynamic balancing
    • Aircraft equations of motion
    • Stability derivatives
    • Stability quartic
    • Phugoid motion and short-period oscillations
  • Unit 5.0: Lateral and Directional Stability and Control I
    • Yaw and side-slip relationships
    • Dihedral effect and component contributions
    • Lateral control: aileron control power
    • Strip theory
    • Aileron reversal
    • Weathercock stability and directional control
  • Unit 6.0: Lateral and Directional Stability and Control II
    • Rudder requirements and dorsal fin effects
    • One-engine inoperative condition
    • Dynamic stability: Dutch roll, spiral divergence, directional divergence
    • Autorotation and spin

Text/Reference Books:

  1. Aircraft Performance and Design John D. Anderson McGraw Hill Education, Indian Edition 2017
  2. Flight Stability and Automatic Control, Robert C. Nelson McGraw-Hill Education, Second Edition, 1997
  3. Performance, Stability, Dynamics, and Control of Airplanes, Bandu N. Pamadi, AIAA Education Series, Third Edition, 2015
  4. Airplane Performance, Stability and Control Courtland D. Perkins and Robert E. Hage, John Wiley and Sons, 1966
  5. Aircraft Performance Theory and Practice Martin E. Eshelby, Elsevier; First Edition 2012
