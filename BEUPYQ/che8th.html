Instrumentation Process & Control Lab [114801P]

  • List of Experiments: (Perform all experiments)
    • To calibrate and study the response of bimetallic thermometer
    • To study the response of a liquid level tank system
    • To calibrate the P/I converter
    • To calibrate the given manometer for level measurement
    • To determine the viscosity of given samples by constant stress rotational viscometer
    • To study and calibrate diaphragm control valve
    • To study the response of first order system in series using two tank liquid level system (non-interacting system) to step input
    • To study the response of first order system in series using two tank liquid level system (interacting system)
    • To calibrate a thermocouple using a glass bulb thermometer
    • To assess the accuracy of a pressure gauge with a dead weight gauge tester

Transport Phenomenon [114802]

  • Module 1: Unit-1.0 (6 hrs.)

    • Pressure and temperature dependence of viscosity
    • Theory of viscosity of gases (low density) and liquids
    • Convective momentum transport
    • Shell momentum balances
    • Boundary conditions
    • Selected applications
  • Module 2: Unit-2.0 (8 hrs.)

    • Navier stokes equation
    • Use of equation of change to solve steady state flow problems
    • Comparisons of laminar and turbulent flows
    • Time smoothed equations of change for incompressible fluids
    • The time smoothed velocity profile near a wall
    • Turbulent flow in ducts and jets
  • Module 3: Unit-3.0 (8 hrs.)
    • Fourier's law of heat conductions
    • Temperature and pressure dependence of thermal conductivity
    • Thermal conductivity of gases, liquids, solids and composite solids
  • Module 4: Unit-4.0 (8 hrs.)
    • Shell energy balance
    • Boundary conditions
    • Heat conduction with an electrical heat source
    • Nuclear heat source
    • Viscous heat source
    • Chemical heat source
    • Composite walls and fins
    • Forced convection
    • Free convection
  • Module 5: Unit-5.0 (6 hrs.)
    • Fick's law of diffusions
    • Analogy with heat & mass transfer
    • Transport by molecular motion
    • Shell mass balance and boundary conditions
    • Temperature and pressure dependence of diffusivities
    • Concentration profile for stagnant gas film

  • Module 6: Unit-6.0 (6 hrs.)

Program Elective- III: Recent Trends in Chemical Engineering [114806]

  • Module 1: Unit-1.0 Sustainable and Green Chemical Processing (7 hrs.)
    • Sustainability: Concept, Overview and attributes of sustainability
    • Green Chemistry and 12 Principles of Green Chemistry
    • Case studies to demonstrate the concept of atom economy and E-Factor
    • Numericals based on estimation of atom economy and E-factor
    • Integration of Ecofriendly Practices: Green approach in raw material processing
    • Unit processes and Unit operations
    • Alternate pathway case studies (Ibuprofen and Propylene oxide synthesis)
    • Shift from Chemical transformation to biochemical transformation (Biodiesel and acrylamide manufacturing)
  • Module 2: Unit-2.0 Circular Economy and Waste Minimization (6 hrs.)
    • Linear Economy
    • Circular Economy and Industrial Ecology
    • 5R approach for waste minimization relevant examples
    • Waste to resource conversion: Waste valorization (Flowsheet and Process description)
    • Pyrolysis of tyres and plastics
    • 2G ethanol manufacturing from waste biomass
    • Zero Liquid discharge: Concept, component and process flow
  • Module 3: Unit-3.0 Chemical Process Intensification and Advanced Separation (6 hrs.)
    • Process intensification Concept and approach of intensification
    • Microreactors and Membrane reactor: Constructional features and applications
    • Microwave, Cavitation and Photochemical based intensification: Features and applications
    • Advanced separation processes: Reactive distillation, Supercritical fluid extraction and pervaporation
    • Heat Integration Network and its application in Energy optimization
  • Module 4: Unit-4.0 Nanotechnology and Advanced Material (10 hrs.)
    • Nanotechnology: Introduction and its significance in chemical engineering
    • Physical, chemical, Electrical and optical properties of nanomaterials
    • Classification examples and applications of nanomaterials on the basis of: Dimensions: Zero, one, two and three dimensional
    • Origin: Natural and engineered
    • Pore size: Microporous, mesoporous and macroporous
    • Composition: Carbon based, inorganic, organic and composites
    • Introduction and features of Advanced materials such as Metal Organic Framework (MOF)
    • Smart and Surface responsive materials
    • Application of above materials in: Catalysis, Energy and hydrogen storage, Drug delivery, Carbon capture, As a sensor that responds to changes in pH. temperature or chemical nature
  • Module 5: Unit-5.0 Digital Chemical Engineering - Industry applications (8 hrs.)
    • Journey from Industry 1.0 to 4.0
    • Digital Twins: Concept, components like physical assets (reactors, distillation columns, process vessels, etc.), IIoT sensors, visual model and data link
    • Component Digital twin like IIoT Sensors Data logger, real time data integration and analysis tools
    • Data driven Physics based Simulation model that represent process/equipment
    • Controllers and visualization dashboard equipped with AR
  • Module 6: Unit-6.0 Digital Chemical Engineering AI applications (5 hrs.)
    • Al applications in Chemical Engineering
    • Process Optimization
    • Reaction Prediction
    • Process Fault Detection
    • Quality Control
    • Process Simulation and Modeling
    • Energy Optimization

    • Hydrocracking Process
    • Catalytic Reforming Process

    • Petrochemical from C4 chemicals: (a) Butadiene (b) Methyl tertiary butyl ether, (c) Butyl acetate
  • Module 6: Unit 6.0 Aromatics (6 hrs.)
    • Separation of aromatic hydrocarbon (BTX) from reformate by using Udex process
    • Aniline manufacturing process from hydrogenation of nitrobenzene
