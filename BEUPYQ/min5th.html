    • Pre-Mining And Induced Stresses
    • Pressure Arch Theory
    • Stress Distribution Around The Single Excavation, Circular, Multiple, Pillar, And Irregular Shapes
    • Analytical Approaches: Numerical Models, FEM, BEM, ANSYS

  • Unit 6.0: Rock Supports
    • Theory Of Rock Mass Reinforcement By Rock Bolting, Its Types And Cable Bolting, Shotcreting
    • Mechanism Of Support, Forces And Displacements Associated With Bolting
