Industrial Fluid Flow Operation Lab

List of Experiments

  1. Determination of viscosity of different liquids by using given redwood viscometer at constant temperature.
  2. Determination of viscosity of a given liquid at different temperature by using redwood viscometer.
  3. Measurement of differential and gauge pressure using given manometer.
  4. Calculation of energy associated with fluid using Bernoulli's principle.
  5. Determination of Co-efficient of discharge for venturi meter to calculate theoretical & experimental value.
  6. Determination of Co-efficient of discharge for orifice meter to compare theoretical & experimental value.
  7. Calibration of Rotameter for given flowrate to plot curve for area Vs flowrate.
  8. Determination of type of flow (Laminar/Turbulent) using Reynolds apparatus.
  9. Determination of discharge coefficient for notches & weirs.
  10. Determination of coefficient of viscosity for given viscous fluid to verify stokes's law.
  11. Determination of head loss due to sudden expansion & sudden contraction.
  12. Calculation of viscosity of given fluid by hagenpoiseuille equation.
  13. Determination of characteristics curve of Centrifugal pump.
  14. Plot the head vs flowrate curve for reciprocating pump.
  15. Plot flow rate & head relation for Gate valve (different pipe diameter).
