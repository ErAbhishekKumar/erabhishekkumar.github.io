Dynamics of Machinery [162501]

  • Unit 1.0: Force analysis of mechanism
    • Dynamics of plane motion of a rigid body
    • Dynamically equivalent two mass system
    • Correction torque
    • Forced in mechanism and machines
  • Unit 2.0: Turning moment diagram
    • Fluctuations of crankshaft speed and energy in a direct acting engine mechanism
    • Flywheels
  • Unit 3.0: Cams
    • Classification of cams and followers
    • Types of followers and retardation
    • Cam profile and generation of concentric and offset radial cam profiles by graphical method
    • Cams with specified contours tangent Cam with roller follower
    • Circular arc Cam with flat follower
  • Unit 4.0: Analysis of gyroscopic motion
    • Principle of gyroscope
    • Gyroscopic couple and gyroscopic reaction couple
    • Gyroscopic effects on the movement of ships
    • Aeroplanes
    • Two wheeled and four wheeled vehicles
    • Gyrostabilizers
  • Unit 5.0: Effects of inertia of reciprocating masses on engine frame
    • Unbalanced primary and secondary forces and couples
    • Balancing of primary and secondary forces
    • Partial balancing of locomotives
    • Balancing of multicylinder in line and radial engines
    • Direct and reverse cranks methods for balancing of radial engines
  • Unit 6.0: Mechanical vibrations
    • Basic concepts degree of freedom
    • Types of damping and viscous damping
    • Natural free
    • Damped free and damped forced vibrations of a single degree of freedom spring mass system
    • Reciprocating and rotating unbalance
    • Vibration isolation and transmissibility
    • Whirling of shaft
    • Elementary treatment of two degree of freedom systems torsional vibrations of single rotor and two rotor systems
    • Transverse vibration of simply supported beam energy method
    • Rayleigh's and Dunkerley method

Dynamics of Machinery Lab [162501P]

Perform all experiments:

  • Determination of inertia forces
  • Piston effort
  • And turning moment for a given crank speed
  • Experimental/graphical evaluation of correction torque for uniform speed operation
  • Construction of turning moment diagram and calculation of flywheel energy fluctuations
  • Determination of moment of inertia of a flywheel and coefficient of fluctuation of speed
  • Construction of cam profiles for different follower motions (SHM, Uniform velocity, Uniform acceleration)
  • Graphical construction of cams with specified contours (roller follower, Flat-faced follower)
  • Determination of gyroscopic couple and verification of gyroscopic principles
  • Static and dynamic balancing of rotating masses using balancing apparatus
  • Study of primary and secondary unbalanced forces in single-cylinder or multi- cylinder engines
  • Determination of natural frequency
  • Damping factor
  • And transmissibility for free and forced vibrations
