Lab Subjects

Fluid Mechanics [116401]

  • Unit-1.0: Properties of Fluids and Fluid Statics
    • Introduction And Classification Of Fluid
    • Types Of Fluids: Ideal, Real, Newtonian, Non-Newtonian, Plastic
    • Fluid Properties: Density, Specific Gravity, Specific Weight, Specific Volume
    • Dynamic And Kinematic Viscosity
    • Surface Tension And Capillarity
    • Vapour Pressure And Compressibility
    • Bulk Modulus
    • Fluid Statics: Pressure, Fluid Pressure, Pressure Head, Pressure Intensity
    • Concept Of Absolute Vacuum, Gauge Pressure, Atmospheric Pressure, Absolute Pressure
    • Pressure Measurements: Simple And Differential Manometers
    • Mechanical Pressure Gauges And Relative Equilibrium
    • Hydrostatic Equation
    • Forces On Plane And Curved Surfaces
    • Buoyancy And Metacentre
  • Unit-2.0: Fluid Dynamics
    • Lagrangian And Eulerian Descriptions
    • Classification Of Fluid Flows
    • Flow Visualization: Path Line, Stream Line, Streak Line
    • Forms Of Energies: Potential Energy, Kinetic Energy, Pressure Energy, Total Energy
    • Concept Of Datum Pressure, Velocity And Total Head Of Fluid In Motion
    • Stream And Potential Functions, Flownets
    • Governing Equations: Continuity Equation, Momentum Equation, Energy Equation
    • Euler's Equation And Bernoulli's Equation
    • Applications Of Bernoulli's Equation
  • Unit-3.0: Flow through Pipes and Boundary Layer-II
    • Flow Measurement: Definition And Types Of Orifices
    • Vena Contracta, Coefficient Of Contraction (Cc), Coefficient Of Discharge (Cd), Coefficient Of Velocity (Cv)
    • Experimental Determination Of Cc, Cd, Cv
    • Construction, Working And Application Of Venturimeter, Orifice Meter, Pitot Tube, Nozzle
    • Viscous Flow: Concept Of Viscosity
    • Reynolds Number And Its Criteria For Plate And Pipes
  • Unit-4.0: Flow through Pipes and Boundary Layer-I
    • Darcy-Weisbach Equation
    • Loss Of Head Due To Friction In Pipe
    • Hagen-Poiseuille Formula
    • Reynold's Experiment
    • Laminar Flow Through Circular Conduits
    • Friction Factor And Moody Diagram
    • Major And Minor Losses
    • Hydraulic And Energy Gradient Lines
    • Pipes In Series And Parallel
    • Boundary Layer Concepts And Types Of Boundary Layer Thickness
  • Unit-5.0: Dimensional Analysis and Model Studies
    • Fundamental Dimensions And Dimensional Homogeneity
    • Rayleigh's Method And Buckingham Pi Theorem
    • Dimensionless Parameters
    • Similitude And Model Studies
    • Distorted And Undistorted Models
  • Unit-6.0: Turbines and Pumps
    • Impact Of Jets And Velocity Triangle
    • Classification Of Turbines And Working Principles
    • Pelton Wheel, Francis And Kaplan Turbine
    • Selection Of Turbine Based On Head And Discharge
    • Classification Of Pumps: Centrifugal Pumps Working Principle, Velocity Triangles, Work Done By Impeller
    • Total Head Of Pump, Classification, Impellers, Casing, Stages, Priming And Cavitation
    • Reciprocating Pumps: Construction, Working Principle, Single Acting And Double Acting, Slip, Negative Slip, Use Of Air Vessels
    • Comparison Of Centrifugal And Reciprocating Pump

Propulsion-I [116402]

  • Unit 1.0: Basics of Air-Breathing Propulsion
    • Introduction To Air-Breathing And Non-Air-Breathing Engines
    • Aircraft Engines: Piston Engines And Gas Turbine Engines (Jet Engines)
    • Laws Of Physics Applied To Propulsion
    • Types Of Propulsion Used In Aircrafts And Spacecrafts
    • Convection Heat Transfer: Free Convection From Vertical Flat Plate, Planes, Cylinder, Sphere, Enclosed Space
    • Effect Of Laminar And Turbulent Flow On Convection, Combined Free And Forced Convection
    • Aircraft Piston Engines: Principal Components, Crank Case, Crankshaft, Camshafts, Sump, Accessory Gearbox, Cylinder, Pistons, Connecting Rods, Valves
    • Internal Combustion Engine Process: SI And CI Engines, 4-Stroke And 2-Stroke Engines, Air Standard Cycles
    • Multi-Cylinder Aircraft Engine Arrangements, Merits, Efficiencies, Cooling, Lubrication, Ignition Systems, Valve Timing Diagrams
    • I.H.P, B.H.P, S.H.P, Performance, Effect Of Altitude, Power Required, Power Available, Supercharging
  • Unit 2.0: Aircraft Gas Turbine Engines (Aircraft Jet Engines)
    • Principal Components: Inlet, Compressor, Combustor, Turbine, Nozzle
    • Gas Turbine Cycle Analysis: Air Standard Brayton Cycle, Actual Cycle, Component Efficiencies
    • Cycle Analysis: Single Spool, Multi-Spool, Afterburner, Free Turbine
    • Propeller Theories: Types, Thrust Momentum Theory, Blade Element Theories, Design And Selection
    • Jet Propulsion: Working Of Gas Turbine Engine, Thrust Equation, Factors Affecting Thrust, Thrust Augmentation
    • Performance Characteristics: Efficiency, Thrust, Specific Fuel Consumption
  • Unit 3.0: Aircraft Gas Turbine Engine Components: Inlets, Combustors, and Nozzles
    • Classification Of Inlets, Compressor Inlet Ducts, Ice Protection, Inlet Design, Subsonic And Supersonic Inlets, Ram Effect
    • Internal Flow, Stall, Boundary Layer Separation, Diffuser Performance
    • Supersonic Inlets: Starting Problem, Shock Swallowing, External Deceleration
    • Combustors: Chemistry Of Combustion, Combustion Equations, Process, Chamber Geometry, Classification, Burners (Simplex, Duplex)
    • Materials, Chamber Design, Performance, Operating Variables, Fuel Injector, Flame Tube Cooling, Ignition, Flame Stabilization
    • Aircraft Fuels, HHV, LHV, Orsat Apparatus
    • Nozzles: Convergent, Divergent, Variable Area, Noise Reduction, Thrust Reversers, Nozzle Flow, Choking, Efficiency, Losses
    • Shock Waves, Rayleigh Flow, Fanno Flow, Effect Of Frictional Duct Length
  • Unit 4.0: Aircraft Gas Turbine Engine Component: Compressor
    • Axial And Centrifugal Compressors: Construction, Stages, Operating Principles
    • Euler's Turbomachinery Equations, Single-Stage Axial Compressor: Velocity Triangle, Work, Compression Ratio, Performance
    • Cascade Action, Multi-Stage Compressor, Degree Of Reaction, Diffusion Factor, Stage Loading, Flow Coefficient
    • Design Process, Compressor Efficiencies: Stage, Adiabatic, Polytropic
    • Free Vortex And Constant Reaction Designs, Fan Balancing, Stall And Surge, Air Flow Control: Bleed Valves, Variable Guide Vanes, Stator Vanes
    • Centrifugal Compressors: Velocity Triangle, Performance Characteristics
  • Unit 5.0: Aircraft Gas Turbine Engine Components: Turbine
    • Expansion Process, Constructional Details, Materials, Axial Flow Turbine, Blade Element Theory, Velocity Triangles, Power Output
    • Multi-Staging, Turbine Cooling, Heat Transfer, Stage Parameters, Degree Of Reaction, Stage Temperature And Pressure Ratio
    • Blade Spacing, Radial Variation, Velocity Ratio, Free Vortex Theory, Limiting Factors, Performance, Turbine And Compressor Matching
    • Radial Turbines, Losses And Efficiency, Fuel Systems, Lubricating Systems, Secondary Air Systems, Starting Systems
  • Unit 6.0: Other Propulsion Systems
    • Types: Turbojet, Turbofan, Turboprop, Turboshaft, Performance Comparison, Bypass Ratio, Dual Shaft Engines
    • Gross Thrust, Net Thrust, Choked Nozzle Thrust, Thrust Horsepower, Equivalent Shaft Horsepower, SFC, Engine Efficiencies
    • Parametric Cycle Analysis Of Ideal Engines: Ramjet, Turbojet With/Without Afterburner, Turbofan, Turboprop
    • Real Engine Cycle Analysis, Ramjet, Scramjet, Pulsejet Components And Working, Engine-Aircraft Matching, Environmental Aspects

Aircraft Structure-I [116403]

  • Unit-1.0: Aircraft Structures and Materials-I
    • Strength To Weight Ratio, Stiffness To Weight Ratio
    • Loads On Aircraft: Structural Nomenclature, Ground Loads, Air Loads, Load Factor, Aerodynamic Loads, Symmetric Manoeuvre Loads
    • Pure Translation, Inertia Forces On Rotating Bodies, Load Factors For Translational And Angular Acceleration
  • Unit-2.0: Aircraft Structures and Materials-II
    • Metallic And Non-Metallic Materials: Aluminum Alloy, Titanium, Stainless Steel, Composites
    • Desirable Properties For Aircraft Application, Temperature Variations
    • Basic Structural Elements: Wing And Fuselage Constructions
    • Stress-Strain Curve For Mild Steel And Aluminium, True Stress And Engineering Stress
  • Unit-3.0: Statically Determinate & Indeterminate Structures
    • Statically Determinate And Indeterminate Structures (Trusses, Beams)
    • Truss Analysis By Method Of Joints, Single And Double Redundancy
    • Shear Center, Kinematic Indeterminacy, Clapeyron's Theorem Of Three Moments, Moment Distribution Method For Indeterminate Beams, Landing Gear
  • Unit-4.0: Energy Methods of Structural Analysis
    • Strain Energy In Axial, Bending, Torsion And Shear Loadings
    • Castigliano's Theorems And Applications To Statically Indeterminate Systems
    • Elastic Theorems: Superposition, Maxwell's Reciprocal Theorem
    • Energy Methods Applied To Beams, Frames, Rings, Trusses
    • Principle Of Virtual Work: Dummy Load And Unit Load Methods
  • Unit-5.0: Failure Theories
    • Ductile And Brittle Failure, Multi-Load Situations
    • Maximum Principal Stress Theory, Maximum Shear Stress Theory, Maximum Principal Strain Theory, Maximum Strain Energy Theory, Maximum Distortion Energy Theory (Von-Mises)
    • Graphical Representation, Failure Theories For Brittle Materials: Mohr-Coulomb And Modified Mohr-Coulomb
  • Unit-6.0: Structural Analysis of Wing & Fuselage
    • Semi-Monocoque Structures, Distribution Of Concentrated Loads In Webs
    • Loads On Fuselage Bulkhead, Analysis Of Wing Ribs, Direct Stress And Shear Flow Distribution In Wing Spars, Tapered Wing And Fuselage Frames

Fluid Mechanics Lab [116401P]

List of Experiments

  1. Use Viscometer To Determine The Viscosity Of A Given Liquid.
  2. Measure The Rise Of Liquid Level Using Capillary Action In Capillary Tube.
  3. Determine The Specific Gravity Of Any Given Fluid.
  4. Use Manometer/Incline Manometer To Measure The Pressure Of The Given Fluid.
  5. Determine Experimentally And Theoretically The Center Of Pressure.
  6. Determination Of Metacentric Height Of A Floating Body.
  7. Experimentally Justify Bernoulli's Theorem For A Viscous And Incompressible Fluid.
  8. Determine The Hydrostatic Force Due To Water Acting On A Partially Or Fully Submerged Surface.
  9. Determine Reynold's Number In Different Flow Conditions.
  10. Determine Discharge Through A Given Pipe Using Orifice Meter, Pitot Tube And Venturi Meter.
  11. Determine The Coefficient Of Discharge (Cd), Velocity (Cv), And Contraction (Cc) Of Various Types Of Orifices.
  12. Calibrate The Given Orifice Meter And Study Variation Of Cd With Reynolds Number.
  13. Calibrate Venturi Meter And Study Variation Of Cd With Reynolds Number.
  14. Determine Discharge Coefficients Of V-Notch And Rectangular Notch.
  15. Determine The Minor Head Loss Coefficient For Different Pipe Fittings.
  16. Investigate The Reaction Force Produced By Impact Of A Jet Of Water On Target Vanes.
  17. Plot The Characteristics Curves Of The Given Centrifugal Pump.
  18. Plot Characteristics Curves Of The Given Reciprocating Pump.
  19. Plot Characteristics Curves Of The Given Pelton Wheel.
  20. Plot Characteristics Curves Of The Given Francis/Kaplan Turbine.
