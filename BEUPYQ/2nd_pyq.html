Question Frequency Guide

The number of stars indicates exactly how many times a question (or a similar question) has appeared in recent years.

Repetition Logic: Across all available papers of same subject, Questions are considered repeated if their text (including options, if any) matches 60% or more.

Why not 100% match? Exact match logic often fails in semester exams due to different numerical values or minor logic manipulations.
This matching threshold (60%+) ensures we capture all conceptually similar questions.
