Mine Hazard and Rescue [113701]

  • Unit 1.0: Introduction
    • Classification Of Mine Hazards: Physical, Chemical, Mechanical, Environmental, And Human-Related
    • Common Causes Of Accidents In Mines: Roof Fall, Inundation, Explosion, Fire, Machinery Accidents, Etc
    • Accident Statistics And Trends In Indian And Global Mining Sectors
    • Accident Investigation, Root Cause Analysis, And Reporting Procedures
    • DGMS Accident Classification And Definitions (Serious, Fatal, Dangerous Occurrence)
  • Unit 2.0: Mine Fires, Explosions, And Inundations (9 hrs)
    • Causes And Types Of Mine Fires: Surface, Underground, Spontaneous Heating
    • Detection, Prevention, And Control Of Mine Fires
    • Learn About Fire Detection, Alarm Systems, And Communication Protocols
    • Explosion Hazards: Firedamp, Coal Dust, And Their Preventive Measures
    • Practice Use Of Firefighting Equipment: Extinguishers, Hoses, And Self-Breathing Apparatus (SBA)
    • Inundation: Causes, Precautions During Working Near Old, Waterlogged Areas, Protective Barriers, And Drainage Methods
    • Case Studies Of Major Mine Disasters (E.G., Chasnala, Raniganj, New Kenda)
  • Unit 3.0: Mine Gases, Ventilation Emergencies, And Environmental Hazards
    • Introduction To Mine Gases, Properties, Detection, And Physiological Effects Of Mine Gases (CH₄, CO, CO₂, H₂S, NOx, Etc)
    • Ventilation System Failures And Emergency Response
    • Heat, Humidity, Noise, Dust, And Vibration Hazards: Control And Monitoring
    • Gas Detection Equipment, Alarms, And Continuous Monitoring Systems
    • Occupational Health Hazards And Environmental Management In Mines
  • Unit 4.0: Mine Rescue Organization And Equipment
    • Outline Rescue Team Structure And Roles: Captain, Gasman, Mapman, First Aiders
    • Roles And Responsibilities Of Rescue Personnel And Team Composition
    • Types Of Breathing Apparatus: Self-Contained, Self-Rescuer, Oxygen Apparatus, And Maintenance
    • Rescue Tools And Communication Systems
    • Rescue Operations In Fire, Explosion, And Inundation: Procedures And Protocols
    • First Aid In Mine Accidents And Casualty Handling
  • Unit 5.0: Mine Safety Management
    • Principles Of Mine Safety Management Systems (MSMS)
    • Risk Assessment, Risk Matrix, And Control Hierarchy
    • Statutory Provisions: Mines Act, Coal Mines Regulations, Metalliferous Mines Regulations Related To Safety
  • Unit 6.0: Emergency Planning
    • Emergency Preparedness, Disaster Management, And Mock Drills
    • Accident Prevention Through Behaviour-Based Safety (BBS)
    • Documentation And Reporting Of Safety Performance Indicators

Advanced Surface Mining [113702]

  • Unit 1.0: Advanced Surface Mining Methods
    • Review Of Surface Mining Methods: Open Pit, Strip, Terrace And Mountaintop Removal
    • Advanced Stripping Techniques, Multi-Bench And Deep-Pit Mining
    • Continuous Vs. Discontinuous Mining Systems
    • Case Studies: Large Open-Pit Mines (Coal, Iron Ore, Limestone, Metal Mines In India And Abroad)
  • Unit 2.0: Mine Planning And Design
    • Open-Pit Layout: Ultimate Pit Limit (UPL), Pit Slope, Bench Parameters
    • Haul Road Design: Geometric And Structural Design, Safety Considerations
    • Dump And Stockpile Design
    • Mine Design Software Overview (I.E. SURPAC, GEOVIA, Datamine)
    • Pit Optimization Using Lerchs–Grossmann And Floating Cone Methods
  • Unit 3.0: Equipment Selection And Performance Evaluation (8 hrs)
    • Advanced Surface Mining Machinery: Shovels, Draglines, Bucket Wheel Excavators, Surface Miners, Highwall Miners
    • Equipment Selection Based On Productivity And Cost Models
    • Truck–Shovel–Crusher System Optimization
    • Maintenance And Reliability Analysis Of Surface Equipment
    • Automation And AI In Surface Mining Equipment
  • Unit 4.0: Production Planning, Scheduling And Optimization (9 hrs)
    • Short-Term And Long-Term Mine Planning
    • Production Scheduling Using Linear Programming And Network Analysis (PERT/CPM)
    • Grade Control And Blending Strategies
    • Simulation And Digital Twins In Surface Mining
    • Economic Evaluation Of Surface Mines: NPV, IRR, Cash Flow Analysis
  • Unit 5.0: Environmental And Safety Management I
    • Slope Stability And Ground Control In Surface Mines
    • Dust, Noise, Vibration Control, And Water Management
    • Land Reclamation And Mine Closure Planning
  • Unit 6.0: Environmental And Safety Management II
    • Drone-Based Monitoring, Slope Radar, And Remote Sensing Applications
    • Sustainable And Responsible Mining Practices (SDG Alignment)

Program Elective: Mine Acquisition & Operationalization [113704]

  • Unit 1.0: Introduction To Mine Acquisition
    • Overview Of The Mining Sector: Stages From Exploration To Production
    • Classification Of Mineral Resources And Reserves (UNFC & JORC Systems)
    • Concept Of Mineral Block Auctions And Allocation Policies Under MMDR Act
    • Licensing Systems: Reconnaissance Permit (RP), Prospecting License (PL), Composite License (CL), And Mining Lease (ML)
  • Unit 2.0: Legal And Regulatory Framework
    • Overview Of Indian Mineral Legislation And Governance
    • Mines And Minerals (Development And Regulation) Act, 1957 And Latest Amendments
    • Mineral Concession Rules (MCR), 2016; Mineral Auction Rules, 2015
    • Role Of The Ministry Of Mines, The Indian Bureau Of Mines (IBM), And The State DMG In Mine Acquisition
    • Compliance Under DGMS And IBM Guidelines
    • Lease Documentation, Renewal, And Transfer Processes
  • Unit 3.0: Mine Development Planning And Operationalization (9 hrs)
    • Stages Of Mine Development: Pre-Feasibility, Feasibility, And Detailed Project Report (DPR)
    • Preparation Of Mining Plan And Mine Closure Plan As Per IBM Guidelines
    • Statutory Clearances Required: Mining Lease Execution; Environmental Clearance (EC); Forest Clearance (FC); Consent To Establish (CTE) And Consent To Operate (CTO); DGMS Permissions (Safety & Health)
    • Land Acquisition And Rehabilitation (LARR Act, 2013)
    • Infrastructure Development: Roads, Power, Water, Housing, And Communication
  • Unit 4.0: Financial, Economic And Contractual Aspects (8 hrs)
    • Components Of Mine Project Cost And Capital Budgeting
    • Financial Modeling For Mine Development Projects
    • Mine Financing Options: Public-Private Partnerships, Bank Loans, Equity, And FDI
    • Contract Mining, Joint Ventures, And Mine Developer & Operator (MDO) Model
    • Cost Estimation For Overburden Removal, Production, And Logistics
    • Royalties, Taxes, And Statutory Payments (DMF, NMET, Etc)
  • Unit 5.0: Sustainable And Safe Mine Operationalization I (5 hrs)
    • Environmental And Social Governance (ESG) Aspects Of Sustainable Mine Development Practices
    • Health, Safety, And Environmental (HSE) Management Plan For New Mines
    • Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) In Mine Start-Up Areas
  • Unit 6.0: Sustainable And Safe Mine Operationalization II (4 hrs)
    • Monitoring, Reporting, And Compliance Post-Commissioning
    • Recent Trends: Automation, Digitalization, And Green Mining Initiatives

Mine Hazard and Rescue Lab [113701P]

Perform any 10 Experiments:

  • Identify Fire Types, Suppression Methods And
  • Safe Firefighting Practices Underground
  • Detection Of CH₄, CO, CO₂, O₂, H₂S And Combustible Gas Levels
  • Interpretation Of Concentrations Of CH₄, CO, CO₂, O₂, H₂S And Combustible Gas Levels
  • Signs Of Spontaneous Heating And Mitigation Steps
  • Coal Dust Explosibility (Rock Dusting, Stone Dust)
  • Coal Dust Explosibility And Preventive Controls (Rock Dusting, Stone Dust)
  • Plan Protective Measures Against Water Inrush And Establish Emergency Pumping/Evacuation Procedures
  • Mine Communication Methods During Emergencies
  • Test Mine Communication Methods During Emergencies
