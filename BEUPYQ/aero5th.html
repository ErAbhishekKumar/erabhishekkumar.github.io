Theory of Machines Lab [116503P]

Preform any ten Experiments:

  • Draw line diagrams with dimensions of the working models of all the inversions of following mechanisms available in the lab, Slider Crank Mechanism, Double Slider Crank Mechanism, Four bar Mechanism, Any two other mechanisms used in aeronautical applications.
  • Dismantle and assemble, Wiper mechanism of any four-wheeler, Steering Mechanism of Tractor, Slider Crank mechanism of any IC Engine, Any two other mechanisms used in aeronautical applications.
  • Draw the profile of radial CAM without offset for given follower (Knife edge and Roller follower) to obtained desired follower motion. (Minimum two problem on A2 size sheet), Draw the profile of radial CAM to obtained desired follower motion used in aeronautical applications.
  • Draw the profile of radial CAM with offset for given follower to obtained desired follower motion. (Minimum two problem on A2 size sheet).
  • Measure main dimension of a Tangent flank cam and Convex cam used in aeronautical applications and estimate lift, velocity and acceleration at critical points.
  • Measure slack side and tight side tension in Chain of sprocket of an experimental Bicycle setup, Measure slack side and tight side tension in Chain of sprocket of any aeronautical application.
  • Study of various types of gears and gear train specific to aeronautical applications.
  • Find out velocity and gear ratio in, Lathe machine gear box, Cane crushing machine, Two wheelers, Drilling Machine, Any two setups in aeronautical domain with gear mechanism.
  • Calculate the braking torque required for different brakes in different load situations, Identify the use of brakes in aeronautical applications and calculate the braking torque required (in one application) for the given brake in different load situations.
  • Use rope/band brake dynamometer to calculate power in an IC Engine, Identify the use of dynamometer in aeronautical applications and calculate the power required (in one application) for the given dynamometer in different load situations.
  • Dismantle and Assemble an internal expanding brake and Disc Brake, Dismantle and Assemble an internal expanding brake and Disc Brake related to aeronautical application.
  • Dismantle and Assemble following, Single plate clutch, Multi plate clutch, Centrifugal clutch, Any one aeronautical system with clutch.
  • Estimate torque transmitting capacity of single and multi-plate clutch with the help of working model.
  • Perform balancing of many unbalanced rotating masses in single plane using rotating balancing machine, Identify two such situations in an aeronautical setup.
  • Determination of Critical Speed in Whirling of Shafts, Determination of Natural Frequency in Longitudinal Vibrating System, Determination of Natural Frequency in Torsional Vibration System, Determine the radius of Gyration of the given IC engine connecting rod using compound pendulum principal, Determine the Moment of inertial of a given body using Trifilar suspension system, Calculate the dynamic parameters of undamped free vibration of spring mass system, Calculate the dynamic parameters of simply supported beam for different damping, Calculate the dynamic parameters of Undamped torsional vibrations of single and double rotor system, Calculate the dynamic parameters of damped torsional vibration of single rotor system and also determine the damping coefficient, Calculate the dynamic parameters of forced damped vibration of spring mass system.
  • Calculate the gyroscopic couple and other parameters for different angular velocities.
