Professional Practice, Law & Ethics [119701]

  • Unit 1.0 (5 hrs): Professional Practice
    • Respective roles of various stakeholders: Government (constituting regulatory bodies and standardization organizations, prescribing norms to ensure safety of the citizens)
    • Standardization Bodies (ex. BIS, IRC) (formulating standards of practice)
    • Professional bodies (ex. Institution of Engineers(India), Indian Roads Congress, IIA/ COA, ECI, Local Bodies/ Planning Authorities) (certifying professionals and offering platforms for interaction)
    • Clients/ owners (role governed by contracts)
    • Developers (role governed by regulations such as RERA)
    • Consultants (role governed by bodies such as CEAI)
    • Contractors (role governed by contracts and regulatory Acts and Standards)
    • Manufacturers/ Vendors/ Service agencies (role governed by contracts and regulatory Acts and Standards)
  • Unit 2.0 (3 hrs): Professional Ethics
    • Definition of Ethics, Professional Ethics, Business Ethics, Corporate Ethics, Engineering Ethics, Personal Ethics
    • Code of Ethics as defined in the website of Institution of Engineers (India)
    • Profession, Professionalism, Professional Responsibility, Professional Ethics
    • Conflict of Interest, Gift Vs Bribery, Environmental breaches, Negligence, Deficiencies in state-of-the-art
    • Vigil Mechanism, Whistleblowing, protected disclosures
  • Unit 3.0 (6 hrs): General Principles of Contracts Management
    • Indian Contract Act, 1972 and amendments covering General principles of contracting
    • Contract Formation & Law; Privacy of contract
    • Various types of contract and their features
    • Valid & Voidable Contracts
    • Prime and subcontracts; Joint Ventures & Consortium
    • Complex contract terminology
    • Tenders, Request For Proposals, Bids & Proposals
    • Bid Evaluation; Contract Conditions & Specifications
    • Critical /"Red Flag" conditions
    • Contract award & Notice To Proceed
    • Variations & Changes in Contracts; Differing site conditions
    • Cost escalation; Delays, Suspensions & Terminations
    • Time extensions & Force Majeure; Delay Analysis
    • Liquidated damages & Penalties; Insurance & Taxation
    • Performance and Excusable Non-performance
    • Contract documentation; Contract Notices
    • Wrong practices in contracting (Bid shopping, Bid fixing, Cartels)
    • Reverse auction; Case Studies
    • Build-Own-Operate & variations; Public-Private Partnerships
    • International Commercial Terms
  • Unit 4.0 (6 hrs): Arbitration, Conciliation and ADR (Alternative Dispute Resolution) system
    • Arbitration - meaning, scope and types - distinction between laws of 1940 and 1996
    • UNCITRAL model law - Arbitration and expert determination
    • Extent of judicial intervention
    • International commercial arbitration
    • Arbitration agreements - essential and kinds, validity, reference and interim measures by court
    • Arbitration tribunal - appointment, challenge, jurisdiction of arbitral tribunal, powers, grounds of challenge, procedure and court assistance
    • Award including Form and content, Grounds for setting aside an award, Enforcement, Appeal and Revision
    • Enforcement of foreign awards - New York and Geneva Convention Awards
    • Distinction between conciliation, negotiation, mediation and arbitration, confidentiality, resort to judicial proceedings, costs
    • Dispute Resolution Boards; Lok Adalats
  • Unit 5.0 (3 hrs): Engagement of Labour and Labour & other construction-related Laws
    • Role of Labour in Civil Engineering
    • Methods of engaging labour- on rolls, labour sub-contract, piece rate work
    • Industrial Disputes Act, 1947
    • Collective bargaining
    • Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946
    • Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923
    • Building & Other Construction Workers (regulation of employment and conditions of service) Act (1996) and Rules (1998)
    • RERA Act 2017, NBC 2017
  • Unit 6.0 (5 hrs): Law relating to Intellectual property
    • Introduction - meaning of intellectual property, main forms of IP, Copyright, Trademarks, Patents and Designs, Secrets
    • Law relating to Copyright in India including Historical evolution of Copy Rights Act, 1957, Meaning of copyright - computer programs, Ownership of copyrights and assignment, Criteria of infringement, Piracy in Internet - Remedies and procedures in India
    • Law relating to Patents under Patents Act, 1970 including Concept and historical perspective of patents law in India, Patentable inventions with special reference to biotechnology products, Patent protection for computer programs, Process of obtaining patent - application, examination, opposition and sealing of patents, Patent cooperation treaty and grounds for opposition, Rights and obligations of patentee, Duration of patents - law and policy considerations, Infringement and related remedies

Computer Application in Construction Engineering and Management [119702]

  • Unit 1.0 (7 hrs): Introduction to Computer Applications and Project Planning
    • Overview: Role of computers in the modern construction industry; impact of information technology
    • Fundamentals: Computer basics, hardware/software, operating systems overview as relevant to construction applications
    • Project Management Fundamentals: Introduction to project phases, stakeholders, and management functions (planning, scheduling, cost, quality, safety)
    • Scheduling Techniques: Manual methods of bar charts and Network diagrams (CPM/PERT) as a basis for computer application
    • Software Introduction: Overview of common construction management software (e.g., MS Project, Primavera P6)
  • Unit 2.0 (7 hrs): Project Planning and Scheduling Software
    • Software Application: Detailed exposure to planning and scheduling software (e.g., MS Project, Primavera)
    • Key Functions: Creating project networks, defining activities, assigning durations, estimating activity times, and identifying the critical path
    • Outputs and Analysis: Generating Gantt charts, network diagrams, and reports
    • Optimization: Calculating floats, analyzing project duration, and understanding time-cost trade-offs
  • Unit 3.0 (7 hrs): Resource Management and Cost Estimation
    • Resource Planning: Resource identification, allocation, leveling, and management using software tools
    • Cost Estimation: Principles of cost estimation and control; use of spreadsheets and dedicated software for quantity take-off and cost estimation
    • Financial Management: Introduction to construction accounting, cost control techniques, and management information systems (MIS)
    • Earned Value Management: Using software to track and control project costs and performance
  • Unit 4.0 (7 hrs): Specialized Software Applications in Construction
    • Building Information Modeling (BIM): Fundamentals of BIM, creating building information models, and its role in project management
    • Structural and Design Software: Overview and application of software like STAAD Pro or similar for structural analysis and design simulation
    • CAD Applications: Use of Computer-Aided Design in construction documentation and planning
  • Unit 5.0 (7 hrs): Legal and Risk Management, Data & Systems
    • Contract and Legal Aspects: Understanding the legal implications of contracts in the construction context, aided by information systems
    • Risk Management: Application of software in identifying, analyzing, and mitigating project risks
    • Database Management: Fundamentals of databases in managing large-scale construction data sets; data management in a networked environment
    • Emerging Technologies: Introduction to advanced topics like geographic information systems (GIS), GPS, and automation in construction
  • Unit 6.0 (7 hrs): Case Studies and Lab Exercises
    • Integrated Project Exercise: Hands-on application of learned software for a complete case study project (planning, scheduling, resource, cost, and BIM modeling)
    • Software Integration: Understanding data exchange and integration between different software platforms
    • Evaluation: Analyzing various project scenarios, updating project plans based on progress, and generating comprehensive reports for decision-making

  3. Construction Management by James B. Fullman

Optimization Techniques [119704]

  • Unit 1.0 (7 hrs): Introduction to Optimization Techniques
    • Overview of optimization problems- unconstrained vs. constrained, Single variable vs multivariable Optimization
    • Types of optimizations - linear, nonlinear, discrete, continuous
    • Linear Programming: Formulation of linear programming problems, Graphical method for solving LP problems, Simplex method and its applications, Two Phase method
  • Unit 2.0 (7 hrs): Convex Functions
    • Definition and Basic Properties, First-order and Second-order Conditions, Epigraph and Sublevel Sets
    • Concave Functions, Definition and Basic Properties, First-order and Second-order Conditions, Hypograph and Superlevel Sets
  • Unit 3.0 (7 hrs): Hessian Matrix and Eigenvalues
    • Computation of Hessian Matrix, Eigenvalues and Definiteness, Using Eigenvalues to Determine Convexity or Concavity
    • Bordered Hessian Matrix- Second-order Conditions for Constrained Optimization
    • convex optimization problems, Quadratic Programming
  • Unit 4.0 (7 hrs): Introduction to nonlinear optimization problems
    • Types of nonlinear programming problems
    • Single variable optimization: Unimodal Function, Elimination Methods- Unrestricted search, Exhaustive Search
    • Multivariable optimization with no constraints, Multivariable Optimization with Equality Constraints, Multivariable Optimization with Inequality constraints
  • Unit 5.0 (7 hrs): Unconstrained Optimization Techniques
    • Introduction, Direct Search Methods, Random Search Methods
    • Indirect search (Descent) methods, Gradient of a function, Steepest Descent(Cauchy) Method, Newton's Method
  • Unit 6.0 (7 hrs): Modern Methods of Optimization
    • Introduction, Genetic Algorithms- Use case, Simulated Annealing- Use case
    • Particle Swarm Optimization- Use case, Ant Colony Optimization- Use case
    • Optimization of Fuzzy Systems- Use case, Neural-Network-Based Optimization- Use case

Design of Structural Systems [119708]

  • Unit 1.0 (7 hrs): Functional Requirements
    • Defining the purpose of the structure, user needs, architectural constraints, and performance criteria (safety, serviceability)
  • Unit 2.0 (7 hrs): Structural Scheme Selection
    • Evaluating alternative structural systems (frame, truss, shell, etc.), material selection (steel, concrete, wood), and overall configuration
  • Unit 3.0 (7 hrs): Formulation of Design Criteria
    • Establishing design loads (dead, live, wind, seismic, etc.) based on codes, defining acceptable safety factors, and material properties
  • Unit 4.0 (7 hrs): Preliminary Proportioning & Modeling
    • Initial sizing of structural elements (beams, columns, slabs) and creating the structural model for analysis, including computer-aided formulation
  • Unit 5.0 (7 hrs): Analysis of Response
    • Application of structural analysis methods (manual and computer-aided) to determine internal forces, stresses, deflections, and stability
  • Unit 6.0 (7 hrs): Cost, Value & Optimization
    • Economic analysis of different schemes, value engineering, whole-life costing, and achieving an optimal, safe, and economical final design
